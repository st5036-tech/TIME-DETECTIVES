CASE FILE #104: THE MISSING TROPHY

The school trophy disappeared at exactly 4:00 p.m. Several people were doing different activities around school at that exact moment. Detective teams must analyze the past continuous evidence to reconstruct what was happening!

⚠️ RULE: Do not name a culprit without sufficient proof! Focus on verifying exact times and past actions.

Verified Timeline & Evidence
  • 3:50 p.m. Anna was cleaning the classroom.
  • 3:55 p.m. Mark was playing basketball outside.
  • 4:00 p.m. The teacher was talking to another student.
  • 4:00 p.m. Two students were walking near the school office.

Challenge A: Identify the Action

Inspect the witness evidence cards. Choose the correct Past Continuous sentence describing each activity.

Challenge B: Grammar Investigation

Demonstrate command of Past Continuous structures (Affirmative, Negative, Questions, and Interrupted Actions with when / while).
